Conference paper:

- Zero-shot evaluation of two System One models (Jev and Clef) on three-class sentiment analysis in 8 European languages: English, German, French, Czech, Albanian, Bosnian, Croatian and Serbian.
- Comparison on the same test items with five LLMs evaluated zero-shot by Kastrati et al. (2026).

After the conference:

- Evaluation across all 34 languages, including high-, medium- and low-resource languages across diverse linguistic and cultural contexts.
- Assessment of System One models under zero-shot and few-shot settings to measure generalization across languages.
